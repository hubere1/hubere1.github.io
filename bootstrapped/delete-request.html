Request deletion of your data

for the app “Bootstrapped” (Android) · Status: 12 October 2026

In short

  • The app has no account. Your game save is on your device, and most data you can delete there yourself (section 1).
  • Anything held by us or by service providers, you request by email (section 2). We reply within one month at the latest.

1. Delete it yourself

WhatHowNote
Game save, settings, purchase status on the deviceAndroid: Settings › Apps › Bootstrapped › Storage › Clear dataPurchased packs stay with Google Play. With internet access, the app reconciles them again at the next start.
Game save inside the appSettings › “Reset game”Archives the old save on the device instead of deleting it. To delete it, use the row above.
Game statisticsSettings › Change consent (ads & statistics)Where the app shows no form, there is no opt-out in the app. Then clearing the app data is the only option (row above). A new installation ID is created afterwards.
Advertising IDAndroid: Settings › Privacy › AdsYou can reset or delete the advertising ID there. The path may differ by Android version.

2. Request by email

Write to eliashuber1803@gmail.com. The link opens a pre-filled email. You can edit it before sending.
