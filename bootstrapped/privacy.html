2. Data on your device

The app stores the following in its private storage on your device:

  • your save game (including the company name you enter) and a backup copy,
  • your settings (e.g. language, appearance, notifications, offer suggestions),
  • the status of your purchases (Founder Pack, Style pack) and a random customer ID from RevenueCat (see section 6),
  • daily counters for ad videos (for the visible daily limits),
  • a randomly generated installation ID (see section 5),
  • a technical error log.

This data is not transferred to us. We do not run a server. Storing it is necessary for the game (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021 (TKG 2021)).

You can delete all of it in Android (Settings › Apps › Bootstrapped › Storage › Clear data) or by uninstalling the app. “Reset game” in the app archives the old save on the device instead of deleting it.

3. Consent

For consent, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (standard: IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). Where required – in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland – the app asks on first launch, before any ad is loaded. Your choice is stored on your device. For gameplay statistics the app asks separately (section 5).

You can change or withdraw it at any time: Settings › Change ad consent. Withdrawal applies to the future. The game remains fully playable without consent.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app only shows rewarded video ads, and only when you tap an offer. There are no banners and no ads that start on their own.

Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

Data: When a video is loaded or shown, Google processes in particular your IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), device and app information (e.g. model, Android version, language, app version) and your interaction with the ad (viewed, cancelled, tapped). Google may store and read identifiers on your device for this purpose.

Purposes: delivering ads, limiting frequency, measuring performance, preventing fraud and – only with consent – personalising ads.

Legal basis: with consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) TKG 2021. Without consent, Google serves at most limited, non-personalised ads; we base the processing needed for that on our legitimate interest in funding the free app through voluntary ads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If Google cannot deliver an ad, the offer is simply unavailable.

Transfers to the USA: Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR).

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (usually under Privacy › Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services · Google Privacy Policy

5. Gameplay statistics (GameAnalytics)

To understand where players get stuck and to improve game balance and offers, the app sends gameplay events to GameAnalytics.

Provider: GameAnalytics ApS, Denmark. GameAnalytics processes the data on our behalf (processor, Art. 28 GDPR).

Data:

  • a random installation ID (not the advertising ID, no name, no email) and a random session ID,
  • time, Android version and app version,
  • gameplay events, e.g. session started, tutorial step reached, milestone reached, ad video offered or watched, shop opened, pack purchased (without payment details),
  • for technical reasons, your IP address during transmission.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). After the tutorial the app asks you once “Send game statistics?” and only sends data after “Yes” – the same in every country. Without an answer or after “No”, nothing is sent. You can change or withdraw this at any time under Settings › Send game statistics.

The data is used for analysis only. We do not build advertising profiles from it and do not pass it on. If you clear the app’s data or reinstall the app, a new installation ID is created; the old data can then no longer be linked to your device.

Storage location: GameAnalytics stores the data with cloud providers (Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud). Where data is processed outside the EU, this is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Learn more: GameAnalytics privacy FAQ

6. Purchases (Google Play, RevenueCat)

You buy the Founder Pack and the Style pack through Google Play. Google handles payment, invoicing and refunds; we never receive your payment details.

To verify, unlock and restore purchases, the app uses the RevenueCat service. The app also stores the purchase status on your device so that packs you bought keep working without internet. If Google refunds a purchase, the app locks the pack again on the next start.

Provider: RevenueCat, Inc., USA. RevenueCat processes the data on our behalf (processor, Art. 28 GDPR).

What data:

  • a random customer ID created on first start (no name, no email, no account),
  • your purchases in this app: product, Google Play purchase token, price and currency, date and status (e.g. refunded),
  • technical details: app and Android version, language and country, IP address when connecting.

When: when the app starts, when you buy and when you tap “Restore purchase” in the settings. This happens regardless of your consent to ads and statistics, because purchases would not work otherwise. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Storage location: RevenueCat stores the data in the USA (Amazon Web Services, Snowflake). The transfer is based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Learn more: RevenueCat privacy policy

In the Google Play Console, Google provides us with order information (e.g. order number, product, price, country, date). We use it for accounting, taxes and refunds. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR. The purchase itself is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Android backup

If you have turned on backup to your Google Account on your device, Android backs up the app’s save game and settings (not the error log) so you can restore them on a new device. Google does this as part of your Google Account; we have no access to it. You can turn backup off in Android settings.

8. Notifications

Reminders (e.g. “offline cap reached”) are created by the app directly on your device. No data is sent and no push service is used. The app only asks for the Android permission when you turn notifications on; you can turn them off in the app or in Android.

9. Error reports and contact

“Copy error report” in the settings copies the technical log to your clipboard. Nothing is sent – you decide whether to send it to us.

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message (including any log) to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). We delete the message once your request is resolved, unless a legal retention obligation applies.

We receive crash reports in aggregated form, without reference to you, via the Google Play Console (Android vitals), provided you have allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data with Google.

10. What the app does not do

  • no user account and no sign-in,
  • no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files,
  • no server or database of our own about you,
  • no selling of data.

11. Security

Data only leaves your device encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Save game and settings are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot access. Since we do not run a server, we hold no central collection of your data.

13. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).

Right to object: Where we process data based on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21).

To exercise these rights, email us at the address above. Because we do not keep accounts, we usually cannot link data held by Google or GameAnalytics to a specific person; the quickest way is therefore the controls in the app and in Android (change ad consent, switch off game statistics, clear app data, reset advertising ID). We find and delete your purchase history at RevenueCat using the order number from your Google Play receipt (starts with “GPA.”).

The simplest way to submit a request is via this page.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live (Art. 77). The authority responsible for us is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, Austria (dsb.gv.at).

The app is intended for adults (18 and over).
